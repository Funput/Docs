## Not sure which one to pick? \{#choose\}

Look at your screen and find the box that matches it best.

<Chooser>
<ChoiceRow look="gnome" title="GNOME on Ubuntu or Fedora" hint="A thin bar along the top of the screen">

Pick [IBus](./ibus.mdx) for the fewest steps. Pick [Fcitx5](./fcitx5.mdx) if you often type in WPS Office.

</ChoiceRow>
<ChoiceRow look="kde" title="KDE Plasma" hint="Kubuntu, Fedora KDE, openSUSE, KDE neon">

Pick [Fcitx5](./fcitx5.mdx). KDE Plasma has a built-in place to turn on Fcitx5 in System Settings.

</ChoiceRow>
<ChoiceRow look="other" title="Other desktops" hint="Linux Mint, Xubuntu, MATE and the rest">

Pick [Fcitx5](./fcitx5.mdx), the recommended choice for most machines.

</ChoiceRow>
</Chooser>
